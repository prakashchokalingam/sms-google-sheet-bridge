Notices

SMS Google Sheet Bridge reads incoming SMS on this phone, masks codes, and uploads
batches to a Google Sheet that you control.

Third-party services
- Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Apps Script are services of Google.
- Messages travel over the network to your Google account.
- Your carrier may charge for data.

What is stored
- Skipped messages are never stored on the phone and never uploaded.
- Masked messages are stored on the phone with [REDACTED] in place of codes.
- The phone deletes each batch after a successful upload.
- Your Google Sheet keeps the uploaded rows until you delete them.

What to expect
- Redaction reduces risk. It cannot guarantee that every secret is removed.
- Review your rules and test samples before you rely on them.
- Do not use SMS Google Sheet Bridge for messages you are not allowed to forward.
- Keep the Web app URL and the shared secret private.
